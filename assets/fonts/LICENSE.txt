WHY THESE ARE HERE
------------------
GroveEngine ships no font of its own: BitmapFont's built-in 8x8 bitmap is a fallback, and a game is
expected to supply its own face. These two are provided so the engine's demos and tests have a REAL
face to render with out of the box, and so a game has a sane default to start from.

Apache 2.0 permits redistribution, including in a commercial game, with attribution. A game shipping
these must carry this notice. Swap in your own face by pointing `fontPath` (renderer config) or
`render:font {path}` at it — nothing here is hardcoded.

Source: the bgfx dependency's example runtime assets (deps/bgfx/bgfx/examples/runtime/font/), copied
here so the engine does not depend on a third party's example folder staying put.
